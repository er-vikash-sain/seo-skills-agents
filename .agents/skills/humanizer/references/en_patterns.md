# English AI Writing Patterns (33-Pattern Reference Guide)

Based on Wikipedia's *Signs of AI Writing* guide by WikiProject AI Cleanup.

## 1. Content & Inflated Significance Patterns

| # | Pattern | Words / Phrasing to Watch | Before (AI Slop) | After (Humanized) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Significance Inflation** | *stands as, serves as a testament, pivotal role, crucial milestone, evolving landscape, deeply rooted, setting the stage for* | "The company was established in 2010, marking a pivotal moment in the evolving landscape of cloud security." | "The company was founded in 2010 to provide cloud security services." |
| 2 | **Notability Name-Dropping** | *cited in NYT, BBC, FT, featured across major media outlets* | "Her research has been cited in NYT, BBC, and FT, underscoring her authority." | Keep specific contextual citations only; trim vague lists. |
| 3 | **Superficial -ing Analyses** | *highlighting..., reflecting..., showcasing..., contributing to..., cultivating...* | "The tool provides real-time dashboards, showcasing team efficiency and reflecting modern work trends." | "The tool provides real-time dashboards for monitoring team efficiency." |
| 4 | **Promotional & Marketing Fluff** | *nestled in, vibrant, rich cultural heritage, groundbreaking, breathtaking, must-visit* | "Nestled in the heart of Tech Valley, the solution boasts groundbreaking capabilities." | "Located in Tech Valley, the software processes data in real time." |
| 5 | **Vague Attributions** | *Industry reports suggest, Experts argue, Observers have noted* | "Experts argue that automated SEO is crucial for modern growth." | Name specific source or cite exact research paper. |
| 6 | **Formulaic Challenges** | *Despite challenges... continues to thrive, amidst navigating complexities* | "Despite market challenges, the company continues to thrive and innovate." | State actual revenue/growth numbers or drop boosterism. |

## 2. Language & Structural Patterns

| # | Pattern | Words / Phrasing to Watch | Before (AI Slop) | After (Humanized) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | **AI Vocabulary Clichés** | *delve into, tapestry, beacon, testament, paramount, multifaceted, realm, foster* | "Let us delve into the multifaceted realm of cybersecurity." | "Here is how cybersecurity works." |
| 8 | **Copula Avoidance** | *serves as, features, boasts, offers a wide range of* | "The platform serves as a powerful solution for analytics." | "The platform provides analytics." |
| 9 | **Negative Parallelisms** | *It is not just X, it's Y; Not only does it do A, but also B* | "It's not just a database; it's a foundation for growth." | "It is a database built for scale." |
| 10 | **Rule of Three Overuse** | Triplets like *innovation, dedication, and excellence* | "We deliver speed, accuracy, and efficiency across teams." | State specific outcomes naturally. |
| 11 | **Em-Dash Overuse** | Excessive `—` punctuation breaking sentence flow | "Our system—built for enterprise needs—delivers results—fast." | Replace with clean commas, periods, or short sentences. |
| 12 | **Boldface Overuse** | **Key Advantage**, **Core Benefit** on every line | "**Efficiency:** High speed. **Quality:** Premium." | Convert into readable prose paragraphs. |
| 13 | **Formulaic Introductions** | *In today's fast-paced digital world...* | "In today's fast-paced digital world, SEO matters more than ever." | "Effective SEO drives organic traffic." |
| 14 | **Formulaic Outros** | *In conclusion..., To sum up..., Ultimately, only time will tell...* | "In conclusion, adopting AI tools is key to staying ahead." | End with concrete next steps or summary bullet points. |
