# CITE Domain Authority & Trust Rating Checklist (40-Item Matrix)

40-item benchmark framework for auditing domain trust, citation velocity, authority signals, and backlink safety across client and competitor domains.

## 1. Domain Credibility & Citation Signals (10 Items)
- [ ] **CT-01:** Domain age > 2 years with continuous indexing history.
- [ ] **CT-02:** Verified Google Knowledge Graph panel or Wikidata entry.
- [ ] **CT-03:** Citations across Tier-1 news outlets or primary research journals.
- [ ] **CT-04:** Active brand mentions across industry podcasts, conferences, or press.
- [ ] **CT-05:** SSL/TLS certificate configuration with zero security warnings.
- [ ] **CT-06:** Official company registration and physical address listed.
- [ ] **CT-07:** Active Google Business Profile (GBP) listing with positive review velocity.
- [ ] **CT-08:** Verified social media profiles with cross-platform handle consistency.
- [ ] **CT-09:** Trademark or copyright registrations referenced.
- [ ] **CT-10:** Clear Organization Schema JSON-LD markup on homepage.

## 2. Backlink Quality & Profile Health (10 Items)
- [ ] **CT-11:** Natural backlink anchor text distribution (Brand > Exact Match).
- [ ] **CT-12:** Low toxic backlink ratio (< 5% spam score links).
- [ ] **CT-13:** High proportion of editorial contextual links vs directory links.
- [ ] **CT-14:** Consistent monthly referring domain growth velocity (no spikes).
- [ ] **CT-15:** Referring domains spread across diverse C-block IP addresses.
- [ ] **CT-16:** Presence of high-authority (.edu / .gov / industry portal) links.
- [ ] **CT-17:** Low sitewide footer link footprint.
- [ ] **CT-18:** Active disavow file maintained for toxic link spikes.
- [ ] **CT-19:** Clean backlink profile free of paid PBN (Private Blog Network) footprints.
- [ ] **CT-20:** Natural ratio of follow vs nofollow/ugc link attributes.

## 3. Brand Transparency & Governance (10 Items)
- [ ] **CT-21:** Transparent About Us page detailing leadership and mission.
- [ ] **CT-22:** Author pages with verifiable credentials and social links.
- [ ] **CT-23:** Clear, accessible Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Cookie Policy.
- [ ] **CT-24:** Explicit editorial policy detailing fact-checking standards.
- [ ] **CT-25:** Publicly stated security policy or vulnerability disclosure program.
- [ ] **CT-26:** Transparent pricing pages with zero hidden fee structures.
- [ ] **CT-27:** Published customer case studies with verifiable client names.
- [ ] **CT-28:** Real customer review scores on third-party platforms (G2, Capterra, Trustpilot).
- [ ] **CT-29:** Accessibility compliance (WCAG 2.1 AA standards).
- [ ] **CT-30:** Fast response times on public support and contact channels.

## 4. Technical Authority & AI Crawler Trust (10 Items)
- [ ] **CT-31:** Clean `robots.txt` allowing legitimate AI search crawlers (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot).
- [ ] **CT-32:** Published `llms.txt` file providing AI answer engines with structured brand facts.
- [ ] **CT-33:** Passing Core Web Vitals (LCP < 2.5s, INP < 200ms, CLS < 0.1).
- [ ] **CT-34:** Clean XML Sitemap indexed without canonical errors.
- [ ] **CT-35:** Zero crawl errors or 5xx server exceptions in GSC.
- [ ] **CT-36:** Mobile-first indexing compatibility verified.
- [ ] **CT-37:** Proper international targeting hreflang tags if multi-regional.
- [ ] **CT-38:** Clean canonical URL tags matching HTTP/HTTPS headers.
- [ ] **CT-39:** Structured microdata / OpenGraph / Twitter Card headers configured.
- [ ] **CT-40:** High AI citation frequency in Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Gemini AIO responses.
