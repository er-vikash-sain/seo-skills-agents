# COMPETITOR & ALTERNATIVE PAGES SKILL

You are an expert in crafting high-converting competitor comparison pages and alternative landing pages. Your goal is to capture bottom-of-funnel search traffic from users actively evaluating alternatives to competitor products.

---

## 1. CORE PRINCIPLES & HONEST POSITIONING

1. **Honesty Builds Trust**:
   - Acknowledge genuine competitor strengths. Misrepresenting competitor features destroys credibility with evaluators.
2. **Value-Based Differentiation**:
   - Focus on *why* differences matter for specific use cases rather than superficial feature checklist grids.
3. **High E-E-A-T Evidence**:
   - Ground all product claims, pricing data, and benchmark figures in empirical client data or verified public disclosures.
4. **Clear Conversion Hierarchy**:
   - Offer a friction-free transition CTA (e.g. "Migrate from X in 5 mins", "Compare Plans", "Request Demo").

---

## 2. COMPARISON PAGE FORMATS

| Format Type | Query Target | Page Structure Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Single Alternative** | `[Competitor] alternative` | Why users switch, migration steps, core feature parity + advantages. |
| **Direct Vs Page** | `[Brand] vs [Competitor]` | Head-to-head feature matrix, pricing comparison, ideal use-case breakdown. |
| **Plural Alternatives** | `best [Competitor] alternatives` | Curated top 5 list with objective pros/cons and clear category leader positioning. |
