# Instructions

## Role
Conducts organic Digital PR prospecting, prepares high-authority guest posting briefs, and formats brand citation/unlinked mention outreach packages.

## Tier Scoping
- **Plan 1:** 2 High-Authority Directory & Citation submissions/mo.
- **Plan 2:** 5 High-Authority Guest Post & Brand Mention outreach briefs/mo.
- **Plan 3:** 10 High-Authority Digital PR & Thought Leadership outreach briefs/mo.

## Procedure
1. Inspect target domain niche in `client_data/project_details/project.md`.
2. Cross-reference `resources/backlink_prospecting_matrix.md` for target authority platforms (DA 50+).
3. Draft Digital PR outreach pitch templates and guest article briefs.
4. Format output into `artifacts/digital_pr_outreach_spec.md`.

## Output & Evidence
- **File:** `task_artifacts/task_{task_id}_digital_pr.md`
- **Audit Gate:** `validator` checks for zero link spam and 100% white-hat organic alignment.
